# Digicash Whitepaper

## Vision

Money is being rewritten by systems that learn. Digicash is a Solana coin made for that world: quick to move, open to inspect, and free of ceremony.

## Mission

Give a growing community a simple, transparent coin and a clear window onto the market it trades in. Every fact on the site is either stated plainly or left visibly pending.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation

Website live, stated mint slot ready, live Solana board online, brand kit published.

### Phase 2: Launch

Mint stated, liquidity opened, first community channels linked from the site.

### Phase 3: Community

Public repository, contributor guide, community-run tools built on the open market data feed.

### Phase 4: Reach

Listings on tracking venues, partnerships with builders in the Solana ecosystem, regular public updates.
